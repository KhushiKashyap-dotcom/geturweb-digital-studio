# GetUrWeb Digital Studio

# MASTER WEBSITE DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT PROMPT — GETURWEB

Act as a world-class UI/UX designer, creative director, branding specialist and senior frontend developer.

Design and develop a complete, premium, modern, responsive website for **GetUrWeb**, a startup offering affordable, professional website design and development services to businesses.

The website must look like a professionally designed digital agency website, with excellent visual hierarchy, polished interactions, clean typography and a strong conversion-focused layout.

## 1. Brand Identity

**Brand Name:** GetUrWeb

**Instagram:** @geturweb_

**Core Services:**
- Business Website Design
- Website Development
- Restaurant & Café Websites
- Portfolio Websites
- Landing Page Design
- Small Business Websites
- Responsive Website Development

**Brand Colour Palette (STRICTLY FOLLOW):**

- Primary Forest Green: #103D30
- Secondary Sage Green: #70977C
- Background Warm Ivory: #F5F1E8
- Supporting Text: Charcoal / Dark Neutral
- White: #FFFFFF (only where necessary)

Use warm ivory as the dominant background, forest green for important headings, buttons and strong visual elements, and sage green for subtle highlights and supporting accents.

Do not introduce random bright colours.

## 2. Overall Design Direction

Create a website inspired by premium independent creative studios and modern digital agencies.

The design should be:
- Minimal and sophisticated.
- Premium but approachable.
- Modern and visually engaging.
- Clean, spacious and editorial.
- Highly readable on mobile devices.
- Professional without looking corporate or boring.

Use generous whitespace, balanced spacing, elegant typography, subtle borders, refined shadows and carefully designed website mockups.

Avoid:
- Generic AI website templates.
- Excessive gradients.
- Unnecessary icons.
- Cartoon illustrations.
- Random floating elements.
- Overloaded sections.
- Excessive animations.
- Generic stock photography.
- Huge meaningless paragraphs.

## 15. UI/UX & Development Requirements

- Fully responsive across mobile, tablet and desktop.
- Mobile-first design approach.
- Smooth but subtle transitions.
- Functional navigation.
- Working mobile menu.
- Functional FAQ accordions.
- Working CTA links.
- Accessible typography and contrast.
- Optimised images and fast loading.
- SEO-friendly page structure.
- Proper heading hierarchy.
- Semantic HTML.
- Clean, maintainable code.
- No broken links or placeholder buttons.
- Consistent spacing and component styles.
- Add appropriate hover and focus states.

Use Framer Motion or subtle CSS animations only where they improve the experience.

## 16. Typography

Use no more than two font families.

Suggested pairing:
- Headings: Manrope or Sora.
- Body: Inter.

Use bold, confident headlines and comfortable body-text spacing.

## 17. Final Design Quality

The final website should feel like a premium creative agency website built by an experienced designer and developer.

The first screen should immediately communicate:
1. What GetUrWeb does.
2. Who it serves.
3. Why someone should choose it.
4. How to get started.

Prioritise conversion, visual consistency, trust and usability.

**MOST IMPORTANT:** Do not sacrifice design quality for unnecessary effects. Maintain the exact GetUrWeb brand palette across every section. The final result must be polished, realistic, premium, mobile-friendly and ready for actual business use.
